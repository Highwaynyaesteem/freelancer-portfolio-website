## 🌟 About

**Freelancer Portfolio Website** is a premium, ready-to-deploy portfolio template crafted specifically for freelancers who want to **stand out and win clients**. Elegant typography, smooth animations, and a dark luxury aesthetic combine to make your work shine.

No coding marathon required — clone, customize, and publish. Your professional brand is live in minutes.

> 💡 **Perfect for:** Designers, developers, writers, photographers, consultants, marketers & any freelancer who wants a portfolio that **converts visitors into clients**.

## 🧩 Project Structure

```
💼 freelancer-portfolio/
├── 🏠 app/                    # Next.js App Router pages
│   ├── page.tsx               # Landing page
│   ├── about/                 # About & skills
│   ├── work/                  # Portfolio gallery
│   ├── blog/                  # MDX articles
│   └── contact/               # Contact form
├── 🎨 components/             # Reusable UI blocks
│   ├── hero.tsx
│   ├── project-card.tsx
│   └── testimonials.tsx
├── 🖼️  public/                # Images & assets
├── 🎨 styles/                 # Tailwind config & globals
├── ⚙️  site.config.ts         # Single-source customization
└── 🧪 tests/                  # Playwright e2e tests
```
